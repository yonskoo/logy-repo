Foundation Principle

Matter Cycles, Energy Flows

Unlike sunlight energy that radiates away as heat, every single atom of carbon, nitrogen, and oxygen on Earth today has been recycled for roughly 4.5 billion years.

⚛️

Closed Chemical System

Earth receives almost zero new matter from space. Every protein, DNA strand, and molecule in your body was once inside ancient rocks, oceans, or primordial organisms.

Conservation of Mass: Σ m(reactants) = Σ m(products)
🔄

Reservoirs & Fluxes

Reservoirs (Sinks): Places where elements are stored (atmosphere, oceans, deep rocks, soil).

Fluxes: The biological and physical processes moving elements between reservoirs.

Flux Rate = Mass / Time
⚡

Interconnected Web

These three cycles never act alone. Photosynthesis links Carbon with Oxygen. Protein synthesis requires both Carbon and Nitrogen. Perturbing one disrupts all three.

Redfield Ratio: 106 C : 16 N : 1 P
Element #6 • Carbon [C]

The Carbon Cycle: The Currency of Life

Carbon forms 4 covalent bonds, allowing complex organic chemistry. Its cycle operates across two vastly different timescales: the fast biological loop and the slow geological loop.

The Fast Biological Loop (Days to Decades)

Driven entirely by biological metabolism between the atmosphere and organisms.

  • Photosynthesis: Plants and phytoplankton pull CO₂ from air/water to build glucose.
    6CO₂ + 6H₂O + hv → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂
  • Cellular Respiration: Animals, fungi, and microbes combust glucose to release energy and return CO₂.
    C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ → 6CO₂ + 6H₂O + ATP

The Slow Geological Loop (Millions of Years)

Regulates Earth's long-term climate thermostat via the lithosphere and deep oceans.

  • Ocean Sinks & Limestone: Dissolved CO₂ forms CaCO₃ shells that compress on the seafloor into limestone rock.
  • Fossil Fuel Traps: Ancient organic biomass buried under intense heat and pressure becomes coal, oil, and gas.
  • Volcanic Return: Subducted rocks melt and release CO₂ through eruptions.
Element #7 • Nitrogen [N]

The Nitrogen Cycle: The Locked Vault

Although N₂ gas makes up 78% of our atmosphere, plants and animals cannot breathe it in. The triple bond (N≡N) is chemically inert and requires biological fixation.

01

Fixation

N₂ → NH₃ / NH₄⁺

Rhizobium bacteria in legume root nodules & lightning break the triple bond into bioavailable ammonium.

02

Nitrification

NH₄⁺ → NO₂⁻ → NO₃⁻

Soil bacteria (Nitrosomonas & Nitrobacter) oxidize ammonium into nitrite, then usable nitrates.

03

Assimilation

NO₃⁻ → Biomass

Plant roots absorb nitrates to build essential amino acids, proteins, and RNA/DNA nucleotides.

04

Ammonification

Waste → NH₄⁺

Fungal and bacterial decomposers convert dead tissues, feces, and urea back into ammonium.

05

Denitrification

NO₃⁻ → N₂ Gas

Anaerobic bacteria in wetlands and sediments convert nitrates back into inert atmospheric N₂ gas.

Element #8 • Oxygen [O]

The Oxygen Cycle: Breath & Shield

Oxygen constitutes 21% of the atmosphere and drives aerobic cellular respiration, but its largest reservoir is locked inside Earth's rocky crust.

🌊

Phytoplankton & Ocean

Over 50% to 70% of Earth’s atmospheric oxygen is produced not by rainforests, but by microscopic oceanic cyanobacteria and diatoms (e.g. Prochlorococcus).

Photolysis: 2H₂O + light → 4H⁺ + 4e⁻ + O₂
🛡️

The Ozone Shield (O₃)

High in the stratosphere, UV radiation splits O₂ into free radicals that combine to form ozone (O₃), shielding life from lethal mutagenic UV radiation.

Chapman Cycle: O₂ + O → O₃
⛰️

The Lithosphere Sink

Over 99.5% of all oxygen atoms on Earth are chemically bonded inside silicate minerals, quartz (SiO₂), and iron oxides (Fe₂O₃) in the crust and mantle!

Mineral Sink: 4Fe + 3O₂ → 2Fe₂O₃ (Rust)
Molecular Synergy

The Grand Nexus: How All Three Meet

These elements do not cycle on isolated tracks. They intersect directly at the cellular and planetary levels.

Carbon ↔ Oxygen

The direct reciprocal engine. Plant photosynthesis absorbs CO₂ and generates O₂; animal and microbial respiration consumes O₂ and emits CO₂.

Photosynthesis ⇌ Respiration

Carbon ↔ Nitrogen

Regulated by the C:N stoichiometry. Plants cannot utilize extra carbon without fixed nitrogen to assemble RuBisCO (the most abundant enzyme on Earth).

RuBisCO Enzyme = Carbon + Nitrogen

Nitrogen ↔ Oxygen

Nitrification strictly requires dissolved oxygen. In oxygen-deprived (hypoxic) dead zones, denitrifying bacteria thrive, consuming nitrates and releasing nitrous oxide (N₂O).

NH₄⁺ + 2O₂ → NO₃⁻ + H₂O + 2H⁺
Human Disruption

Human Disruption & Planetary Boundaries

Industrial civilization has drastically accelerated natural fluxes, destabilizing the equilibrium that took millions of years to establish.

🏭

Carbon Overload

Combusting fossil fuels unlocks ancient millions-year-old carbon reservoirs in seconds, pushing atmospheric CO₂ past 420 ppm and driving ocean acidification.

🌾

Haber-Bosch Nitrogen

Synthetic industrial fertilizer fixed more nitrogen than all natural biology combined. Agricultural runoff causes massive algal blooms and catastrophic aquatic dead zones.

⚠️

Ocean Deoxygenation

Warming ocean waters hold less dissolved oxygen. Dying algal blooms decompose via aerobic bacteria, draining oxygen and creating suffocation zones for marine life.

Final Wrap-up

Summary & Core Takeaways

Everything in Earth's chemistry is recycled. Remember these key points for exams and discussions:

01. Carbon

Cycles between organic sugars and greenhouse gases. Regulated quickly by living bodies and slowly by ocean limestone rocks.

02. Nitrogen

78% of air, but chemically locked. Entirely dependent on bacterial fixation (Rhizobium) and nitrification to feed life.

03. Oxygen

Created largely by ocean phytoplankton. Drives metabolism, shields Earth via stratosphere ozone (O₃), and rests in crust minerals.

"In nature, nothing is created, nothing is lost, everything is transformed." — Antoine Lavoisier
